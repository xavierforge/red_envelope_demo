The spin loop's result is overwritten by `self.amount = original`, so the
release build eliminates the loop as dead code. The &mut self borrow is held
for roughly 100 ns, far too short for 8 threads to overlap, even with a
Barrier start. The runtime borrow flag is in place; this probe just never
hits it. The program was left unchanged on purpose (see task rules); making
the loop observable (e.g. std::hint::black_box on the counter) would need
another rebuild and is a follow-up decision.
